# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students managing study sessions across multiple subjects. They use Soma
during work sessions, typically alone at a desk, to track time by subject,
plan their day, and sustain focus. The primary workflow is: open the app,
pick a subject, start the timer.

## Product Purpose

Soma is a personal study productivity app built around time-blocking and
subject tracking. The timer is the core interaction; the day view, calendar,
canvas, and AI assistant all serve the act of starting and sustaining focused
work. Success looks like a student who opens Soma every study session without
thinking twice.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, spacious. The design should feel like a desk cleared for work:
everything at hand, nothing in the way. Close in spirit to YPT's subject-first
density and muted palette, but with more breathing room and a stronger
typographic voice.

## Anti-references

- Notion / Coda: dense wiki blocks, everything editable, no clear hierarchy
- Google Calendar: brightly colored, corporate, event-grid energy
- Todoist / TickTick: gamified, badge-driven, productivity-coach tone
- Generic SaaS dashboards: metric cards, gradient heroes, over-designed states
- YPT verbatim: same layout density and identical UI patterns without
  differentiation (reference the spirit, not the implementation)

## Design Principles

1. **Subjects are the spine.** Every screen should make it effortless to
   track time against a subject. Don't bury that hierarchy.
2. **Breathing room as personality.** Where YPT packs tight, Soma gives air.
   The whitespace is deliberate, not empty.
3. **The day is a narrative.** The day view reads as a living account of time
   spent, not a static scheduling grid.
4. **AI earns its place.** The AI tab is a first-class feature that surfaces
   context and reduces planning friction, not a chat widget bolted on.
5. **Type has a voice.** Typography choices should feel considered and
   distinctive, not system-default neutral.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Muted palette must maintain sufficient contrast ratios. Reduced
motion respected via prefers-reduced-motion.
